# ☁️ T-Drive: Professional Telegram Cloud Storage

**T-Drive** is a high-performance, premium "Files On-Demand" sync engine that transforms your **Telegram Saved Messages** into a fully functional, unlimited cloud drive. 

Built for users who want the power of OneDrive or Google Drive but with the privacy and unlimited potential of the Telegram API.

---

## 🔥 Key Features

### 🚀 Smart Stubs (Files On-Demand)
T-Drive uses advanced "Smart Stub" technology. All your files appear in your local folder as 1KB shortcuts, saving 99.9% of your disk space. Files only download when you open them and automatically "offload" back to the cloud after 2 minutes of inactivity.

### 🖼️ Seamless Thumbnails
Unlike other Telegram drives, T-Drive generates and caches high-quality thumbnails for your images and videos locally. You can browse your cloud files visually without downloading the full data first.

### 🛡️ Mandatory Update System
Built-in version enforcement ensures that all users are always running the latest, most secure version of the sync engine.

### 🛠️ Professional Sync Logic
- **Move Detection:** Intelligent logic that understands when you move files between folders, preventing duplicates.
- **Invisible Hydration:** Files download silently in the background; no more ugly `.tmp` files cluttering your view.
- **Automatic Garbage Collection:** Tiny cached icons are automatically cleaned up when you delete files, keeping your system lean.

### 🎨 Modern Dark-Theme GUI
A beautiful, sleek dashboard built with CustomTkinter that provides real-time sync status, bandwidth usage, and easy configuration.

---

## 💻 Technology Stack
- **Core:** Python 3.10+
- **API:** Telethon (MTProto Telegram API)
- **UI:** CustomTkinter
- **OS Integration:** Windows (cscript, winreg, shortcuts)
- **Image Processing:** Pillow (PIL)

---

## 🛠️ How to Use
1. **Setup:** Open the T-Drive app and enter your Telegram `API_ID` and `API_HASH`.
2. **Dashboard:** Press **Start Sync** to mount your cloud drive.
3. **Enjoy:** Drag and drop files to upload them. Double-click any "Smart Stub" to view the full file instantly.
